How Much Sun Protection Does a Screened Patio Give?

Last updated: November 2026 · Native Screens, serving South Florida since 1972

People think of screen as bug protection and forget it does real work against our brutal sun, too. It won't turn your patio into a cave — and you wouldn't want it to — but a screened patio noticeably cuts UV and glare. Here's what to realistically expect.

What screen does to the sun

Standard screen mesh blocks a meaningful chunk of direct sunlight and UV just by being a woven barrier. You'll feel it: the space under screen is cooler and less harsh than open sun, furniture and flooring fade slower, and the glare off the pool softens. It's partial shade, not full shade — think "comfortable" rather than "dark."

Color and weave matter

  • Charcoal and darker mesh cut glare better and actually see through more clearly than you'd expect — it's the standard for a reason.
  • Tighter weaves (like no-see-um and privacy mesh) block more sun than standard fiberglass, as a side benefit of blocking smaller bugs.
  • Solar or privacy screen on the sun-facing side can add real shade where the afternoon sun hammers you.

Where this matters most

In South Florida, the west- and south-facing sides take the worst of it. If your patio bakes in the afternoon, I'll often suggest a denser mesh on just those panels — you get the extra shade where you need it without darkening the whole enclosure or killing your view on the other sides.

Screen plus a roof

Screen alone is partial shade. If you want to sit out there in the hardest midday sun, pairing the screen with an aluminum patio roof over the seating area is the move — the roof gives you solid shade, the screen keeps the bugs out, and you get a room you can use all day.

My honest take

Don't buy screen expecting blackout shade — that's not what it's for, and full shade would wall you off from the breeze and the view that make the space worth having. But as a way to take the edge off our sun while keeping it open and airy? It does the job, and a smart mesh choice on the hot side makes a bigger difference than people expect.
