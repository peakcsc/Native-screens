Signs your Hollywood pool cage is due

Hollywood sun, summer storms and salt air near the beach all wear screen down over time. You're probably due if you see:

  • Tears or holes, even small ones that let bugs in
  • Screen that sags or bellies out between the frame
  • Mesh that looks chalky, faded or grey from the sun
  • Screen pulling loose from the frame at the corners
  • Bugs inside the cage with no obvious gap

More detail: 7 Signs Your Pool Cage Needs a Rescreen.

How a rescreen works

  1. Free estimate. Larry looks at your cage, checks the frame, and gives you a price.
  2. Strip it. We pull out the old screen and spline.
  3. Clean the channels. The frame channels get cleaned so the new spline seats properly.
  4. New mesh, pulled tight. Fresh screen goes in panel by panel.

Most rescreens are done in a single day. Larger or storm-damaged cages may take longer, and we'll tell you up front.

Mesh options

  • Standard charcoal fiberglass. The most affordable choice. Charcoal is easier to see through than grey.
  • No-see-um mesh. A tighter weave that keeps out tiny biting bugs a standard screen can let through.
  • Pet-resistant mesh. Heavier and tougher, built to stand up to claws and paws.

Not sure which one? Read No-See-Um vs Standard Screen or ask Larry at the estimate.
