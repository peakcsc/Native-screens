What drives the price

  • Size & height. A large, tall, or panoramic "picture window" enclosure uses more aluminum and labor than a small patio.
  • Frame design & color. Standard, mansard, dome, or gable roof lines; bronze, white, or matte black framing.
  • Mesh type. Standard fiberglass, no-see-um (blocks tiny biting bugs), or pet-resistant screen.
  • Add-ons. An aluminum roof, super gutters, extra doors, or pet doors.
  • Permitting & code. New enclosures must meet Florida building code and wind-load requirements — we handle the permit.

Do you need a permit for a pool enclosure in Broward County?

Yes. New pool and patio enclosures in Broward County and across South Florida require a permit and must be built to Florida code. As a licensed contractor, Native Screens pulls the permit and builds it right — so it passes inspection and stands up to storm season.
