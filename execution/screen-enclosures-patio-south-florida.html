Why enclose your patio in South Florida

Between no-see-ums, afternoon storms, and relentless UV, an open patio sits empty most of the year here. A screen enclosure keeps bugs and debris out, cuts sun glare, and adds a shaded, breezy room you'll actually use — often the best value-per-dollar upgrade a South Florida home can make.

Mesh & frame options

Choose standard fiberglass, tighter no-see-um mesh for biting gnats, or pet-resistant screen, in bronze, white, or matte-black aluminum framing. We build to local wind-load and permitting requirements and handle the permit as a licensed contractor.

Common questions

How much does a screen enclosure cost in South Florida?

Screening in a patio or porch typically runs about $2,000–$9,000 depending on size, frame design, and mesh. See our cost guide or call (954) 966-0163 for a free estimate.

Do I need a permit for a screen enclosure in Broward County?

Yes — new screen enclosures require a permit and must meet Florida code. As a licensed contractor, we handle the permitting.

What's the best screen for keeping out no-see-ums?

A tighter no-see-um mesh blocks the tiny biting gnats a standard screen lets through — a popular choice across South Florida.

Can you enclose an existing covered patio?

Yes — if you already have a roof or slab, we can screen it in to create a bug-free room. We'll assess the structure during a free estimate.

Do screen enclosures add value to a home?

In South Florida, a well-built screen enclosure adds usable living space and is a strong selling point, since buyers expect bug-free outdoor areas.

What is a screen enclosure?

A screen enclosure is an aluminum-framed, screened structure over a patio, porch, lanai, or pool that keeps bugs and debris out while letting in the breeze. Native Screens builds them across South Florida.
