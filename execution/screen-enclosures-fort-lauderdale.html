Fort Lauderdale screen enclosure services

Fort Lauderdale's waterfront living is the whole point of owning a home here — but salt air, afternoon storms, and no-see-ums can make the backyard unusable. A well-built screen enclosure keeps the bugs and debris out while you keep the breeze and the view. We handle it all:

Built for Fort Lauderdale's climate

Waterfront and canal-front homes see more salt-air corrosion on older aluminum frames, and the city's heavy summer rain puts any enclosure's screen and gutters to the test. We use materials built for South Florida and build to local wind-load and permitting requirements — so your enclosure passes inspection and stands up to storm season.
