How Much Do Patio Screens Cost in South Florida?

Quick answer: Screening in a patio in South Florida typically costs $2,000–$9,000, a single torn patio screen panel runs $35–$250, and a patio screen door rescreen is about $85–$350. Final price depends on size, mesh, and frame design.

What drives the price

  • Size & height of the patio — more aluminum and labor.
  • Mesh type — standard fiberglass is cheapest; no-see-um, pet-resistant, and privacy mesh cost more.
  • Frame design & color — bronze, white, or matte black; picture-window styles cost more.
  • Permitting — a new enclosure needs a permit and must meet Florida code (we handle it).

Rescreen vs. new patio screens

If your frame is solid and only the screen tore or faded, a rescreen costs a fraction of a new enclosure — you reuse the frame and only pay for new mesh. See our full enclosure cost guide for more.
