Common Questions

Screen Enclosure FAQ — South Florida

How much does a pool enclosure cost in South Florida?

Pool enclosure pricing in Broward, Miami-Dade, and Palm Beach depends on the size of your pool, the screen type, and the frame design. The best way to get an accurate number is a free on-site estimate — call (954) 966-0163 and we'll come measure and give you an honest quote.

Do you repair just one torn screen panel, or the whole enclosure?

Both. We can replace a single failed panel or rescreen your entire pool cage or patio enclosure. If only one panel is torn, you don't need to redo the whole thing — we'll fix exactly what needs fixing.

Do I need a new frame, or can you reuse my existing one?

In most cases we reuse your existing aluminum frame — rescreening only replaces the screen, not the structure. You're paying for new screen, not a whole new enclosure, which keeps the cost down.

How long does a pool cage rescreen take?

Most rescreens are completed in a single day. Larger enclosures or full rebuilds take longer — we'll give you an exact timeline with your free estimate.

What's the best screen for keeping out no-see-ums and mosquitoes?

For South Florida's no-see-ums, a tighter "no-see-um" mesh blocks bugs a standard screen lets through. We'll recommend the right mesh for your home and how you use your space.

What areas do you serve?

All of South Florida — Broward County (Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs, Davie, Plantation and more), Miami-Dade County, and Palm Beach County (Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach).
