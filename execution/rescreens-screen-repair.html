Rescreens & Pool Screen Repair in South Florida

Torn, sagging, or sun-rotted screens? You usually don't need a new enclosure — you need a rescreen. Native Screens repairs and rescreens pool cages, patios, and screen rooms across South Florida, often in a single day.

When to rescreen

  • Panels are torn or ripped (storms, pets, fallen branches).
  • Screen is sagging or loose and pulling out of the frame.
  • Mesh has gone chalky and grey from years of UV (sun rot).
  • Bugs are getting through even though the frame looks fine.

You reuse your frame

Here's what most people don't realize: rescreening reuses your existing aluminum frame. The frame you already have is almost always fine — it's the screen that fails. So you're only paying for new screen, not a whole new enclosure.

One panel or the whole cage

If just a panel or two failed, we replace only those ($35–$250 per panel). If the screen's at the end of its life across the enclosure, a full rescreen gives you a like-new cage. Either way, we match your existing mesh or upgrade you to no-see-um or pet-resistant screen.

Fast, clean, and done right

Most rescreens are finished in a day. We strip the old screen and spline, clean the frame channels, and install fresh mesh pulled tight — no sagging, no gaps.

Common questions

How much does a pool screen repair or rescreen cost?

A single torn panel is about $35–$250, a screen door is $85–$350, and a full pool cage rescreen in South Florida typically runs $1,200–$4,500. See our cost guide for the full breakdown.

Do you reuse my existing frame?

Yes — rescreening reuses your existing aluminum frame. You only pay for new screen, which is why it costs far less than a rebuild.

How long does a rescreen take?

Most rescreens are completed in a single day. Larger or storm-damaged enclosures may take longer.
