Pool Enclosure Styles: Mansard vs Dome vs Gable

When you build a new pool cage, the roof style changes the look, the height, the cost, and how it sheds South Florida's heavy rain. Here's a plain-English guide to the four common styles so you can pick the right one.

Last updated: November 2026 · Native Screens, serving South Florida since 1972

The four common roof styles

  • Mansard — the most popular. The roof angles in at the edges before flattening on top, giving a clean, finished look and good water runoff. A great all-around choice for most homes.
  • Dome (hip) — angled panels rise to a peak or ridge, adding height and an open, airy feel over the pool. Sheds water and debris well; a bit more material.
  • Gable — a classic peaked "A-frame" ridge, tall and dramatic, often used over larger pools or to match a home's rooflines.
  • Flat / shed — a single low slope, the most economical, common on smaller patios and screen rooms.

How to choose

  • Budget-focused: mansard or flat keep costs down.
  • Want height and a premium look: dome or gable.
  • Heavy rain / debris: peaked styles (dome, gable, mansard) shed water and leaves better than flat.
  • Matching your home: we'll match the frame color (bronze, white, matte black) and line to your architecture.

Every style is built to Florida wind-load code. We'll walk the options on-site and recommend what fits your pool, your home, and your budget.

Frequently asked questions

Which pool enclosure style is most popular in South Florida?

The mansard style is the most common — it gives a clean, finished look, sheds water well, and works for most homes and budgets.

Does roof style affect the price?

Yes — taller peaked styles like dome and gable use more material and labor than a mansard or flat roof, so they cost more.

Can you match the enclosure to my house?

Yes — we match frame color (bronze, white, matte black) and roof line to your home's architecture during the design.
