What a super gutter does

A standard house gutter can't keep up with the sheet of water that pours off a large screen enclosure or patio roof in a Florida downpour. A super gutter is larger, seamless, and integrated into the enclosure's structure, so it catches that volume and channels it to downspouts — away from your slab, foundation, and walkways instead of sheeting over the edge onto you and your deck.

Why South Florida homes need them

  • Heavy rain — our storms dump water fast; oversized capacity prevents overflow.
  • Protects your slab & foundation — directs runoff to proper downspouts.
  • Keeps the entry dry — no waterfall over the screen door.
  • Seamless aluminum — fewer leaks, low maintenance, matches your enclosure.

Common questions

What is a super gutter?

A super gutter is an oversized, seamless aluminum gutter mounted to the beam of a pool enclosure or patio roof. It's built to handle the heavy rain that a standard gutter can't, channeling it to downspouts.

Can you add super gutters to my existing pool enclosure?

Yes — if your current drainage is overwhelmed in heavy rain, we can add super gutters to an existing enclosure. We'll assess the structure and give you a free estimate.

Do super gutters come with downspouts?

Yes — super gutters are tied into downspouts that carry water away from your slab and foundation, so runoff doesn't pool around the enclosure.

What are super gutters made of?

Seamless aluminum, mounted directly to the enclosure beam. Seamless means fewer joints to leak and low maintenance, matched to your enclosure color.

Do super gutters need cleaning?

Occasionally — clearing leaves and debris a few times a year keeps them draining freely, especially under heavy tree cover.
