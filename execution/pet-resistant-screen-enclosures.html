Why pet-resistant screen

  • 7x stronger than standard fiberglass mesh (heavy vinyl-coated polyester), so it resists tearing from claws and jumping pets.
  • Keeps the bugs out — it still screens like normal, just far tougher.
  • Great for lower panels — many homeowners put pet screen on the bottom 2–4 feet where pets reach and standard mesh above to save cost.

Pet doors too

Repair the damage, then protect it

Common questions

What is pet-resistant screen?

A heavy vinyl-coated polyester mesh about 7x stronger than standard fiberglass screen, built to resist tearing from claws and jumping pets while still keeping bugs out.

Do I need pet screen on the whole enclosure?

Usually not — many homeowners put pet-resistant mesh only on the lower panels pets can reach, with standard screen above, to balance toughness and cost.

Can you add a pet door to my pool cage?

Yes — we install a properly sized screen-enclosure pet door in a panel or the screen door so pets can come and go without tearing the mesh.
