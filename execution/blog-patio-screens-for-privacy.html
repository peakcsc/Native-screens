Patio Screens for Privacy: Options for South Florida Homes

Quick answer: Privacy screen mesh blocks the view into your patio or pool cage from outside while still keeping bugs out. It's a denser, darker weave than standard screen, comes in grey or black, and installs just like a normal rescreen.

Last updated: November 2026 · Native Screens, serving South Florida since 1972

How privacy screen works

Privacy mesh is woven tighter and darker than standard fiberglass screen, so from outside it reads as a solid panel during the day, while you can still see out and the breeze still passes through. It's ideal for street-facing panels, close neighbors, or a hot tub area.

Where homeowners use it

  • Lower panels of a pool cage or patio for privacy at seating height.
  • Street- or neighbor-facing sides only, with standard screen elsewhere to keep the view and airflow.
  • Around a hot tub or outdoor shower.

The tradeoffs

  • Less view out and slightly less airflow than standard screen — which is why most homeowners use it only on the panels that need privacy.
  • Daytime privacy is strongest; at night, interior lights reduce it (like any screen).

We'll recommend the right mix of privacy and standard mesh for your home on a free estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Does privacy screen block the view both ways?

It mainly blocks the view in during the day while you can still see out. At night, interior lights reduce the effect, like any screen.

Does privacy mesh reduce airflow?

Slightly — it's a tighter weave. Most homeowners use it only on the panels that need privacy and keep standard screen elsewhere.

Can you add privacy screen to my existing pool cage?

Yes — privacy mesh installs like a normal rescreen, so we can swap specific panels on your existing frame.
