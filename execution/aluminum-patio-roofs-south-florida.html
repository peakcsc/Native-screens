  • Combination roofs — a solid roof section over seating with a screen enclosure around the pool or patio.

Why an aluminum roof in South Florida

Aluminum won't rot, warp, or feed termites the way wood does, and it shrugs off the sun and salt air. A properly engineered aluminum roof adds a genuinely usable shaded room, protects furniture and flooring from UV, and is built to Florida wind-load code to hold up in storm season.

Built to code, with the permit handled

New patio roofs in Broward County and across South Florida require a permit and must meet building and wind-load code. As a licensed contractor, we engineer it, pull the permit, and build it to pass inspection.

Common questions

What's the difference between an insulated and a pan aluminum roof?

An insulated (foam-core) roof has a solid insulated panel that keeps the space cooler and much quieter in heavy rain. A pan roof is a lighter, lower-cost hollow aluminum cover for shade and rain protection.

Do aluminum patio roofs need a permit in Broward County?

Yes — new patio roofs require a permit and must meet Florida wind-load code. As a licensed contractor, we handle the engineering and permitting.

Can you add an aluminum roof to my existing screen enclosure?

Often yes — we can add a solid roof section over part of an existing enclosure. We'll assess the structure and give you a free estimate.

Will an aluminum roof make my patio cooler?

Yes — especially an insulated (foam-core) roof, which blocks sun and keeps the space noticeably cooler and quieter in the rain than an open or pan roof.

Can an aluminum roof attach to my existing house?

Yes — aluminum patio roofs are typically attached to the home and engineered to Florida wind-load code. We handle the design and permit.

What is an aluminum patio roof?

An aluminum patio roof is a solid insulated or pan-style cover attached to your home for shade and storm protection. It won't rot or warp and is engineered to Florida wind-load code.
