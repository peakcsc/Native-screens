Pool Enclosures & Pool Cages in South Florida

A pool enclosure — a.k.a. a pool cage — keeps bugs, leaves, and debris out of your pool while cutting UV and adding a layer of safety. Native Screens has designed and built custom pool enclosures across South Florida since 1972.

What a pool enclosure does for you

  • Keeps bugs out — enjoy the pool without mosquitoes and no-see-ums.
  • Less cleaning — leaves and debris stay out, so you skim less.
  • UV & shade — reduces harsh sun on the deck and water.
  • Safety — adds a barrier around the pool.

Styles & options

We build mansard, dome, and gable roof lines in bronze, white, or matte-black aluminum, with standard fiberglass, no-see-um, or pet-resistant mesh. Add super gutters, extra doors, or an aluminum roof section for covered seating.

New build, replacement, or rescreen?

If your frame is solid and only the screen has failed, a rescreen restores it for a fraction of the cost. If the structure is damaged or you're starting fresh, we build a new code-compliant, wind-rated enclosure and handle the permit.

Built to Florida code

New pool enclosures in Broward County and across South Florida must meet building code and wind-load requirements. As a licensed contractor, we pull the permit and build it to pass inspection and hold up in storm season.

Common questions

How much does a pool enclosure cost in South Florida?

A new screen pool enclosure typically runs about $5,000–$20,000+ depending on size, height, and mesh. See our cost guide or call (954) 966-0163 for a free estimate.

Do I need a permit for a pool cage in Broward County?

Yes — new pool enclosures require a permit and must meet Florida code. As a licensed contractor, we handle the permitting.

Can you replace an old pool enclosure?

Yes — we remove the old structure and build a new, code-compliant enclosure, or rescreen the existing one if the frame is still sound.
