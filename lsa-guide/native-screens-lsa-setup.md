# Google Local Services Ads (LSA) — Step-by-Step Setup for Native Screens
Goal: get "Google Guaranteed" leads coming in. You pay per lead, set your own
budget cap, and can pause anytime.

====================================================================
BEFORE YOU START — have these ready
====================================================================
[ ] Business name: Native Screens
[ ] Business phone: (954) 966-0163  (use a number you ANSWER — missed LSA calls
    hurt your ranking and waste money)
[ ] Business address / service area (South Florida: Broward, Miami-Dade, Palm Beach)
[ ] Florida contractor LICENSE number + a photo/PDF of it
[ ] Proof of general liability INSURANCE (certificate PDF)
[ ] Your EIN / business details
[ ] A credit card for billing
[ ] Owner's info for a background check (Google uses a partner for this)
[ ] Your Google account (the same one that owns your Google Business Profile)

====================================================================
STEP 1 — Start the application
====================================================================
1. On a computer, go to:  ads.google.com/local-services-ads
2. Click "Get started" / "Sign up".
3. Sign in with the Google account that manages your Google Business Profile
   (so your reviews and profile connect automatically).

====================================================================
STEP 2 — Confirm business basics
====================================================================
1. Enter your location/country (United States) and business name: Native Screens.
2. Choose your JOB CATEGORY. Look for the home-services category that fits best —
   likely "Handyman," "Window cleaner/installer," or a home-improvement /
   "Window and door" style category. Pick the closest match to screen/enclosure
   work. (If "screen enclosure" isn't listed exactly, choose the nearest
   home-improvement category — you can refine the specific services next.)
3. Select the specific services you offer from the checklist (repairs,
   installation, etc.).

====================================================================
STEP 3 — Set your SERVICE AREA (controls who sees you)
====================================================================
1. Add the areas you actually want jobs in. Start TIGHT, not county-wide:
   - Start with your core towns: Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines,
     Davie, Miramar, Hallandale Beach, Dania Beach.
   - You can expand to all of Broward / Palm Beach / Miami-Dade later.
   Why tight first: a smaller area = cheaper, closer jobs and less wasted spend
   while you learn what converts.

====================================================================
STEP 4 — Business hours & lead types
====================================================================
1. Set your hours. Leads mostly come in during open hours.
2. Choose how customers reach you: phone calls (and message leads if offered).
   Make sure the phone forwards to someone who ANSWERS — speed wins the job and
   protects your ranking.

====================================================================
STEP 5 — Upload license, insurance & pass screening
====================================================================
1. Upload your Florida contractor license.
2. Upload your liability insurance certificate.
3. Complete the business + owner BACKGROUND CHECK (Google's partner runs it; it's
   free and standard).
4. Submit for review. Approval + the green "Google Guaranteed" badge usually takes
   a few days up to ~2 weeks. You cannot run ads until this clears — this is the
   main wait, so start it today.

====================================================================
STEP 6 — Set your BUDGET (you control the spend)
====================================================================
1. Set a WEEKLY budget cap. Suggested start: $150–$300/week
   (~$600–$1,200/month). Google never charges past your cap.
2. LSA shows an estimate of how many leads that budget may bring. It's an
   estimate, not a promise.
3. You can raise, lower, or PAUSE anytime (e.g. pause when you're slammed with
   work, raise it in slow weeks).

====================================================================
STEP 7 — After it goes live: protect your money
====================================================================
- ANSWER EVERY CALL FAST. Missed/slow responses lower your ranking and burn
  budget. If you can't answer, the missed-call text-back we set up catches them.
- DISPUTE bad leads. If a lead is spam, a wrong number, or outside your service
  area, report it in the LSA dashboard (or the "Local Services Ads" app) and
  Google refunds it. Do this within the allowed window (usually ~30 days).
- Reviews boost your LSA rank too. Keep the review drive going — it helps here
  AND in organic. You can even send review requests from the LSA app.
- Check the LSA app daily for new leads and to mark which became jobs.

====================================================================
QUICK MATH (so you know it's working)
====================================================================
- Example: leads ~$40 each, you close 1 in 4  →  ~$160 per booked job.
- One rescreen covers several leads; one new enclosure (thousands) covers a
  whole MONTH of leads. As long as you answer and close, the math wins.
- Watch two numbers: cost per lead, and your close rate. If close rate is low,
  the problem is follow-up/answering — not the ads.

====================================================================
PITFALLS TO AVOID
====================================================================
- Don't go county-wide on day one (wastes money on far/low-value leads).
- Don't use a phone you won't answer.
- Don't forget to dispute junk leads — that's real money back.
- Don't confuse LSA with regular Google Search Ads. LSA = the "Google Guaranteed"
  pay-per-lead ads at the very top. Start with LSA.
- Don't pause and forget — check it, feed it reviews, and adjust budget weekly.
