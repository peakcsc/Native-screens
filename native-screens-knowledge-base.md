## 3. Services (what we offer)

### Pool enclosures (pool cages)
Custom aluminum-and-screen enclosures around a pool. New builds, replacements, and rescreens. Keeps out bugs, leaves, and debris; cuts UV; adds a safety barrier. Built to Florida code; we pull the permit.

### Rescreens & screen repair
Replacing torn, sagging, or sun-rotted screen on an existing frame. Single panels or a full rescreen. Reuses the existing aluminum frame — you only pay for new screen, not a rebuild. Most rescreens done in a single day.

### Screen enclosures / patio & porch screens / screen rooms / lanais
Screening in a patio, porch, or lanai to create a bug-free, shaded, breezy room. New builds or screening an existing covered patio.

### Aluminum patio roofs
Solid insulated (foam-core) or pan-style aluminum covers for shade and storm protection. Won't rot or warp; built to Florida wind-load code.

### Super gutters
Oversized, seamless aluminum gutters mounted to the enclosure or patio-roof beam to handle South Florida's heavy rain, channeling water to downspouts. New installs or add-ons.

### Screen door repair & replacement
Sliding and swinging screen doors — new mesh, rollers, hinges, latches, kick plates. Often same-day.

### Pet-resistant screens
Heavy vinyl-coated polyester mesh ~7x stronger than standard, for homes with dogs/cats. Usually installed on lower panels. Pet doors available.

### Panoramic / picture-window pool enclosures
Removes mid-height cross-bars on the view side for wide, uninterrupted views. Uses heavier engineered framing; costs more than a standard cage.

### Pool cage painting & restoration
Clean, treat, and repaint an old, chalky, or rust-streaked aluminum frame to look new — a fraction of the cost of replacement.

### Storm / wind damage repair
Repair of torn panels, bent frames, and lifted anchors after storms. We prioritize storm repairs. (Note: screen panels are designed to blow out before the frame fails — a torn panel after a storm is normal and far cheaper than a bent structure.)

### What we DO NOT do
We do NOT install motorized, electric, automatic, retractable, or magnetic patio screens — those are a different product. We do custom aluminum screen enclosures, rescreens, and repair. If a caller wants motorized/retractable screens, politely say that's not a service we offer.

## 5. Materials & options

### Screen mesh types
- **Standard fiberglass (18x14):** most common and affordable. Blocks mosquitoes and normal insects. Charcoal color is standard (clearest view, least glare). Does NOT block tiny no-see-ums.
- **No-see-um mesh (20x20 or tighter):** blocks the tiny biting gnats a standard screen lets through. Recommended near water/canals/coast (most of South Florida). Slightly less airflow.
- **Pet-resistant mesh:** ~7x stronger, resists claws; usually on lower panels.
- **Privacy / solar mesh:** denser weave, blocks the view in and cuts more sun; used on street-facing or sun-facing sides only.

### Frame
- Frames are **aluminum** (bronze, white, or matte black). Aluminum won't rot, warp, or feed termites and lasts 20–30+ years.
- We do NOT recommend wood (rots, termites) or vinyl as a structural frame in South Florida — aluminum is the right choice here.

### Lifespans (important for repair-vs-replace questions)
- **Aluminum frame: 20–30+ years** — often the life of the home.
- **Screen mesh: 5–7 years** in South Florida before UV sun-rot. This is why most "my enclosure is failing" situations only need a rescreen, not a rebuild.

### Roof styles for new enclosures
Mansard (most popular), dome/hip, gable, and flat/shed. Panoramic "picture window" available.
