## ⭐ First: get your DIRECT review link (2 minutes)

The QR code and texts below currently point to a Google **search** for your reviews (works everywhere, but it's one extra tap for the customer). A **direct "write a review" link** drops them straight onto the star-rating box — far higher conversion. To get it:

1. On your phone, open the **Google Maps** app and search **Native Screens**.
2. Open your business → tap **Reviews** → the **"Write a review"** button, OR in the Google Business Profile app tap **"Ask for reviews."**
3. Google gives you a short share link like `https://g.page/r/XXXX/review`.
4. Send me that link and I'll regenerate the QR code and swap it into the texts — and I can add a one-tap "Leave us a Google review" button on the website.

## ✅ Habits that actually move the number

- **Ask every single happy customer, same day.** The ask is the whole game — most people say yes but forget if you wait.
- **Make it one tap.** Direct link > QR > search. Get that direct link.
- **Reply to every review** (good and bad), briefly and warmly — Google rewards engagement and it shows future customers you care.
- **Keep it steady.** A trickle of 2–4 reviews a month beats a burst, and looks natural to Google.
- **Never pay for reviews or only ask happy people in writing** — against Google's rules and can get them removed. Just ask everyone and let the quality speak.
