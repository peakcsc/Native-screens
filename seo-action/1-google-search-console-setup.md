# Google Search Console Setup — Native Screens (DO THIS FIRST)
Why: your 13 new pages can't rank until Google indexes them. GSC is how you tell
Google to crawl them. Free. ~15 minutes. Biggest single SEO action left.

## Step 1 — Add your site
1. Go to: search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with the SAME Google account that owns your Google Business Profile.
3. Click "Add property" → choose the "URL prefix" box (right side).
4. Enter exactly:  https://www.nativescreens.com
5. Click Continue.

## Step 2 — Verify ownership (easiest method)
Google will offer several verification options. Easiest for you:
- If it offers "Google Analytics" or "Google Business Profile" association, use it.
- Otherwise choose the "HTML tag" method:
   1. It gives you a <meta> tag that looks like:
      <meta name="google-site-verification" content="XXXXXXXX" />
   2. COPY that whole tag and send it to me — I'll paste it into your site's <head>,
      push it, and then you click "Verify". (This is the one spot I need from you.)

## Step 3 — Submit your sitemap (the important part)
Once verified:
1. In the left menu click "Sitemaps".
2. In "Add a new sitemap" type:  sitemap.xml
3. Click Submit.
This tells Google about all 16 of your pages at once.

## Step 4 — Request indexing of key pages (optional, speeds it up)
1. Top search bar → paste a page URL (e.g.
   https://www.nativescreens.com/screen-enclosures-fort-lauderdale.html)
2. Click "Request indexing". Repeat for your top 4–5 pages.

## Step 5 — Turn on AI eligibility
In Search Console, look for the "Search generative AI" setting (under Settings or
page experience). Make sure it's set to INCLUDE — this keeps you eligible to appear
in Google's AI Overviews / AI Mode.

## What you get
- Google crawls your new pages → they become eligible to rank.
- You see the real searches people use to find you, and your impressions/clicks.
- After a few weeks there's real data to measure the whole project's results.
